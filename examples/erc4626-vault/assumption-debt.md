# Assumption Debt Examples

| Assumption | Status | Severity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Underlying token behaves like a standard ERC20. | Needs test | high |
| Underlying token does not rebase. | Unresolved | medium |
| Admin actions follow the documented emergency policy. | Accepted risk | medium |
| Direct donations and first-depositor exchange-rate manipulation are not yet mitigated or bounded. | Needs invariant | critical |

Accepted risk stays visible next to unresolved token behavior and donation/inflation debt in the demo.
